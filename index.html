The mapping rules

Colour

Hue communicates emotional temperature and carries the family, following the feelings wheel: gold is fear, purple is surprise. Every state also has a name and an expression.

Eyes

Eyes carry the quickest signal. Their curve, openness and angle follow the emotion rather than being decorative.

Outer ring and motion

Each emotion has a fixed SVG path and a specific animation. Higher tension becomes sharper or more unstable. Lower energy becomes heavier or slower.

Version one uses code-drawn vectors. Figma SVGs can replace each path without changing the emotion model. The token library and the full attribute table live in the repo.
